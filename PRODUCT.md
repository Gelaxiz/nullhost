# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Niilo and friends sharing temporary files, text, and links.

## Product Purpose

nullhost provides a quick, low-friction place to upload, retrieve, and manage temporary shared material.

## Positioning

A personal utility in the Gelaxiz ecosystem, optimized for directness rather than public cloud-storage complexity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve all upload, account, public listing, administration, legal, and client-download behavior. Do not expose secrets or private uploads through presentation changes.

## Brand Commitments

Part of one Gelaxiz ecosystem. Preserve the `nullhost` name and concise, technical voice.

## Product Principles

- Make the primary upload action unmistakable.
- Keep system status and recovery clear.
- Stay compact, fast, and trustworthy.
- Work well with keyboard and mobile input.
