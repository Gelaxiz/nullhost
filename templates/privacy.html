privacy policy

1. data we host
we only store the absolute minimum amount of data required to run the service. this includes:
- the files and text you upload (we just host them momentarily until they expire or are deleted).
- session cookies just to keep you logged in if you have an account.
- basic web server logs (ip address, timestamp) to prevent spam and abuse.

2. how data is used
your data is used solely to provide the file hosting service. we do not sell your data, track your browsing habits across other sites, or use your files for training ai models.

3. data retention
files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers once they reach their expiration date or burn limit. web server logs are routinely purged.

4. third-party sharing
we do not share your data with third parties unless compelled by a valid, legally binding request from law enforcement authorities.

5. cookies
nullhost only uses strictly necessary session cookies. we do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics cookies. by logging into an account, you consent to our use of these essential cookies.
