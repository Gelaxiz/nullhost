about nullhost

nullhost is a small, direct place for sharing temporary files, text, and links with friends. it is built to stay quick, understandable, and out of your way.

~400 gb storage space currently available

what it does

  • files and pastesupload large files or share text from the same lightweight interface.
  • temporary by designchoose expiration, view limits, or burn-after-reading behavior.
  • private when neededprotect individual uploads and collections with passwords.
  • organized collectionsgroup uploads into folders, select several files, or download a collection as a zip.
  • direct sharinguse raw links, downloads, and qr codes without unnecessary steps.
  • device-friendlyinstall the web app and use the interface in dark or light mode.

made by gelaxiz.

source code & self-hosting: github.com/Gelaxiz/nullhost
