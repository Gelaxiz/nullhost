# Gelaxiz Minimal Adaptive System

nullhost uses a compact Archivo utility interface with restrained blue links, soft borders, and clear text hierarchy. Its dark palette uses a near-black canvas and graphite task surfaces; its light palette translates the same hierarchy to cool off-white and white surfaces without changing the product's visual character. Upload clarity and system state outrank decoration.

The initial theme follows the visitor's operating-system preference. A precisely aligned header control reports the active theme and cycles through light, dark, and true-black OLED palettes; that manual choice persists on the device.

Motion is operational: immediate press response, brief panel entrances, and animated progress/status. Repeated tasks stay fast and reduced motion removes positional travel.
